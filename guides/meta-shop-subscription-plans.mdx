---
title: "Manage subscription plan visibility on Facebook and Instagram Shop"
description: "Choose which Dough Dog subscription plans appear on a product in your Facebook and Instagram Shop"
---

When you sync products to Facebook and Instagram, every subscription plan attached to the product is shown to shoppers by default. Use this guide to hide specific plans on a per-product basis.

<Note>
  This guide assumes you use the official **Facebook & Instagram** Shopify app from Meta. If you sync products another way, subscription plans don't appear on your Facebook and Instagram Shop.
</Note>

## Hide or show a plan on a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Facebook & Instagram app">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps** and open **Facebook & Instagram**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the product">
    Go to **Synced products** and open the product you want to edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the subscription plans">
    Click **Change** next to the Facebook & Instagram fields. In the **Update Facebook & Instagram fields** dialog, find the **Subscription plans** section and uncheck any plan you want to hide. Leave a plan checked to keep it visible.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/guides/meta-subscription-plans.png" alt="Update Facebook & Instagram fields dialog showing three subscription plans with checkboxes and their selling plan IDs" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The change applies to this product only.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each plan in the list shows its selling plan ID. Use the ID to tell apart plans with similar display names and match them to the right plan in Dough Dog. See [Selling plans](/platform/shopify/selling-plans) for how Dough Dog creates and syncs those plans.
