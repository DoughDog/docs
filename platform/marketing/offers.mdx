title: "Offers"
description: "Show subscribers discounted products in their customer portal"
---

Offers put a discounted product in front of your subscribers while they're in their customer portal. When a customer accepts an offer, the item is added to their next upcoming order.

Use offers to raise order value, introduce new products, or reward a specific group of customers.

## Create an offer

In the Admin, go to **Marketing → Offers** and click **Create Offer**. Setup has four steps. You can click **Save Draft** at any point and finish later.

### 1. Details

- **Title (Internal Use)**: A name for your team. Customers never see it.
- **Start Date** and **End Date**: Optional. The offer only shows between these dates. Leave either blank to keep that side open.
- **Discount Type**:
  - **None**: The item is offered at its regular price.
  - **Fixed Dollar Discount**: Takes a set amount off the item.
  - **Percentage Discount**: Takes a percentage off the item.
  - **Free Product**: The item is free.
- **Offer Accepted Limit (Optional)**: The total number of times the offer can be accepted before it stops showing. Leave blank for unlimited.

### 2. Items

Add up to 10 products. For each product, choose **All variants** or **Specific variants**. Only the variants you select are offered.

### 3. Segmentation

Choose who sees the offer:

- **All Customers**: Everyone sees it.
- **Use Segment**: Only customers who match your rules see it.

Rules live in groups. In an **AND** group, a customer must match every rule. In an **OR** group, they must match at least one. If you add more than one group, a customer who matches any group sees the offer.

You can have up to 3 groups, with up to 3 rules in each.

| Rule | Matches customers who |
| --- | --- |
| **In Klaviyo Segment** | Are in the Klaviyo segment you choose |
| **Not In Klaviyo Segment** | Are not in the Klaviyo segment you choose |
| **Subscriber Status** | Are Active, Cancelled, or Yet to Subscribe |
| **Active Subscription Count** | Have exactly, or at least, 1, 2, or 3 active subscriptions |
| **Has Subscription (Plan)** | Have an active subscription on the plan you choose |
| **Has Subscription (Product Type)** | Have an active subscription for the product type you choose |
| **Purchased Product** | Have bought the product you choose |
| **Did Not Purchase Product** | Have never bought the product you choose |

Klaviyo rules require your Klaviyo account to be connected.

For example, to target active subscribers who haven't bought your socks yet, create one AND group with **Subscriber Status** set to Active and **Did Not Purchase Product** set to your sock product.

### 4. Design

- **Headline**: Required.
- **Subheading**: A short description of the offer.
- **Offer Image**: If you don't upload one, the first product's image is used.
- **Button Text (Call to Action)**: Required. Defaults to "Accept Offer".
- **Discount Price Additional Text**: A short note shown with the price, such as "Renews at regular price".
- **Confirmation Message**: Shown after the customer accepts. Defaults to "Thank you for your purchase!"

The preview beside the form shows how the offer will look. When you're done, click **Save and Activate**.

## Activate an offer

Before you can activate an offer, it needs a title, at least one product, a headline, and button text.

A product can only be in one active offer at a time. If a product is already in another active offer, deactivate that offer first.

## Manage your offers

The **Offers** page lists each offer with its status and results:

- **Viewed**: How many customers have seen the offer.
- **Accepted**: How many customers have accepted it.
- **# Available**: The accepted limit, if you set one.
- **# Remaining**: How many acceptances are left before the offer stops showing.

Drag offers to reorder them. Customers see their eligible offers in this order.

To take an offer down, open it and click **Deactivate**. It returns to Draft, and you can edit and reactivate it later. You can only delete an offer while it's a draft.

## What customers see

A customer sees an offer when all of these are true:

- The offer is active and today is within its dates.
- The offer hasn't reached its accepted limit.
- The customer matches its segment.
- The customer hasn't already accepted it.
- At least one of its items is in stock.

Out-of-stock variants are hidden. Each customer can accept an offer once, and they need an upcoming order to accept it.

<Note>
  Changes to who sees an active offer can take up to an hour to reach customers. To apply them right away, deactivate and reactivate the offer.
</Note>
